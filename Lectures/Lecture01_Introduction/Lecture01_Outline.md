## Welcome to this Course (10-12 slides)

* Energy generation:
	* At the simplest level, there are two ways we make energy: 
		* Indirect: turn a turbine that has magnets on it --> electricity is generated --> transmit to consumers
			* Turn turbine with matter in the liquid phase (hydro)
			* Turn turbine with matter in the gaseous phase (fossil fuels, nuclear power, geothermal)
		* Direct: photons from the sun hit photosensitive materials that emit electrons that can be gathered to create current --> transmit to consumers
* Ways to generate energy: 
	* Direct: solar power
	* Indirect: fossil fuels, hydroelectric, geothermal, nuclear fission, nuclear fusion
* Give some motivational facts about energy generation starting right here in Buffalo / Niagara region. 
	* AC/DC current wars, Tesla vs Edison, and get a few social media content creators with their takes. 
	* Very first large-scale energy generation project in human history, and it was clean energy! Hydroelectric power! 
* The dark side of Industrialization and energy generation
	* Anthropogenic Climate change
