OLIGARCHY

Everything you need to play the Custom Stake Edition — from setup to the final foreclosure.

1Setup Screen

When you launch the game you land on the setup flow. There are two screens.

Player Count

Click a number button — or press 2 through 6 — to choose how many players will compete. The game supports 2 to 6 players.

Player Details

Each row represents one seat. Fill in a name, set a starting cash amount, and choose Human or Bot.

Name
Up to 16 characters. Used on the HUD and in event messages.
Starting Cash
Any positive whole number. The standard game uses $1,500, but this edition lets you change it freely — try $500 for a brutal scramble or $5,000 for a leisurely build-out.
Type
Human (you control) or Bot (AI plays automatically). Toggle the button to switch. A lit green button means Bot.
Navigation Press Tab to move between fields, Shift+Tab to go back. Press Enter to advance — or to start the game from the last field once all rows are valid.

Click Start Game when every player has a name and a positive cash amount. Click Back to return to the player-count screen.

3Your Turn

Turns follow a fixed sequence. The active player is shown in the top-left of the HUD along with their cash, property count, and position.

1
Roll the Dice — Click Roll Dice or press R. Two six-sided dice determine how many squares you move.
2
Move your token — Your piece travels the board. If you pass Go, you collect $200 immediately.
3
Land action — The game prompts you depending on what square you landed on (see sections below).
4
Player actions — After any landing is resolved, you may Build, Mortgage, or Trade before ending your turn.
5
End Turn — Click End Turn or press E. Play passes to the next player.
Doubles Rolling doubles lets you take an extra turn immediately. Roll doubles three times in a row and you go directly to Jail.

4Buying & Auctions

Buying a Property

When you land on an unowned property, the game asks: Buy it for $X?

  • Buy — pay the listed price, add the deed to your portfolio.
  • Auction — send the property to an open auction (see below).

You cannot buy a property you cannot afford. If your cash is below the price, only the Auction option is available.

Auctions

An auction starts whenever a player declines to buy a property at face value, or when a card sends a property to auction directly. Every player — including the one who declined — may bid.

  • Bids rise in $1 increments. Players take turns in seat order.
  • A player who cannot afford the next minimum bid is dropped automatically.
  • When only one bidder remains with a standing bid, they win the property at their bid price.
  • If all players pass without bidding, the property returns to the bank unsold.
Strategy note Auctions let you acquire properties for less than face value — or force rivals to overpay. Even if you don't want the property, pushing the bid up burns your opponent's cash.

6Houses & Hotels

You can build on a color group only when you own every lot in that group and none of them is mortgaged.

Building Houses

Click Build (or press B) during your Player Actions phase to open the build screen. Select lots to add or remove houses. Building must be even — you cannot put a second house on one lot while another lot in the same group has none.

Each house costs the amount printed on the deed (varies by color group).

Upgrading to a Hotel

Once a lot has 4 houses, the next purchase replaces all four houses with a hotel. A hotel earns the highest rent on the card and costs one additional house payment. Each lot can hold at most one hotel.

Selling Improvements

In the build screen you can also sell houses back to the bank for half their purchase price. Selling must also be done evenly across the group.

Tip The orange and red groups (landing frequently due to their distance from Jail) tend to generate the best return on investment.

7Mortgaging

When you need emergency cash, you can mortgage properties to the bank. Click Mortgage (or press M) during your Player Actions phase.

  • You receive half the property's face value immediately.
  • A mortgaged property collects no rent.
  • You must sell all houses and hotels on a color group before you can mortgage any lot in it.
  • To un-mortgage a property, pay the face value back plus 10% interest.
Important You cannot build on a group if any lot in it is mortgaged. Un-mortgage it first.

8Jail

You go to Jail in three ways: landing on Go to Jail, rolling doubles three times in a row, or drawing a card that sends you there. You move directly to Jail and do not collect $200 for passing Go.

Getting Out

At the start of your turn while in Jail, you are offered three options:

  • Pay the Fine — $50. Pay immediately and roll normally.
  • Use a Jail Card. Spend a Get Out of Jail Free card if you have one.
  • Roll for Doubles. Roll the dice — if you get doubles, you move that many spaces and play normally. No doubles means you stay in Jail (your turn ends, no movement). On your third failed attempt, you must pay $50 and then move.
Jail as shelter Late in the game, with the board loaded with hotels, staying in Jail a few turns can be tactically smart — you collect rent from visitors but avoid landing on expensive squares yourself.

9Chance & Community Chest

Landing on one of these squares draws the top card from the corresponding shuffled deck. The deck is reshuffled once exhausted.

Typical Card Effects

  • Collect / Pay cash — straightforward bank transactions.
  • Advance to a square — move forward (or occasionally backward) to a named location. Passing Go collects $200 unless the card says otherwise.
  • Advance to the nearest Railroad / Utility — and pay double rent if someone owns it.
  • Repairs on your properties — pay a set amount per house and per hotel you own.
  • Get Out of Jail Free — keep this card and use it whenever you need it, or trade it to another player.
  • Go to Jail — move directly to Jail, do not pass Go.

The game always shows you the full card text before asking you to dismiss it. Press Space or click OK to acknowledge and continue.

10Trading

During your Player Actions phase, click Trade (or press T) to open the trade screen. You can exchange any combination of:

  • Properties (you cannot trade buildings — sell them first)
  • Cash
  • Get Out of Jail Free cards

Select the other player, set what each side offers, and send the proposal. The other player may accept or decline. Trades are final once accepted.

Completing a monopoly Trading is the fastest way to break stalemates. Giving up a property you cannot use to complete someone else's monopoly, in exchange for cash or a property you need, often unlocks the whole game.

11Bankruptcy

You go bankrupt when you owe more than you can raise — after selling all your houses, hotels, and mortgaging every property you own — and still cannot cover the debt.

  • If you go bankrupt to another player, all your assets (including mortgaged properties and any Jail cards) transfer to that player. They pay the 10% un-mortgage fee later when they choose to un-mortgage.
  • If you go bankrupt to the bank (from a tax or card), all your properties return to the bank and go back up for auction.

A bankrupt player is eliminated. The game continues until only one player remains.

12Bot Players

Bots are fully automated players — they roll, buy, bid, build, mortgage, and end their turns without any human input. You can mix human and bot players in any combination.

How Bots Decide

Buy?
Buy if cash after purchase stays above $200, and they already own a group member or the price is ≤ 2× the cash floor.
Auction bid
Bid up to 110% of face value if the purchase completes a monopoly, otherwise up to face value — but never below a $200 cash reserve.
Build
Build when cash exceeds a dynamic reserve (at least $300, or 30% of current cash).
Mortgage
Mortgage when cash drops below $150. Un-mortgage when cash is above $800.
Jail
Always pay the fine immediately to keep the game moving.

Speed

Bots act on a 700 ms timer between actions, so you can follow what's happening. A full bot-vs-bot game typically resolves in a few minutes.

Tip Add one bot opponent if you want a solo practice game. Use all bots to watch a game play itself.

13Winning

The last player standing wins. There is no timer and no score — you win by outlasting everyone else through a combination of property acquisition, rent collection, and cash management.

The game displays a Game Over screen showing the winner's name and final holdings.
